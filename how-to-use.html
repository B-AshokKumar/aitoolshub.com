📖 How to Use AI Tools Hub

👋 Welcome

Welcome to AI Tools Hub! This guide will help you learn how to generate AI prompts, explore AI tools, discover prompt templates, stay updated with AI news, and make the most of all the features available in the app.

1️⃣ Generate AI Prompts

Example:

Topic: Solar Energy

Generated Prompt:
Create a professional AI prompt for Solar Energy with detailed explanation and high-quality output.

🎨 Prompt Templates

AI Tools Hub includes multiple professionally designed Prompt Templates to help you generate better AI prompts for different tasks.

Examples of Prompt Templates include Education, Business, Programming, Content Writing, Marketing, Research, Creative Writing, Social Media, Email Writing, and many more.

How to use:

  • Select a Prompt Template that matches your task.
  • Enter your topic in the input box.
  • Tap ✨ Generate Prompt.
  • Review the generated prompt.
  • Tap 📋 Copy Prompt.
  • Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Grok, Microsoft Copilot or any compatible AI assistant.

Different Prompt Templates generate prompts in different styles. Try different templates with the same topic to receive different AI outputs and discover the best results for your needs.

2️⃣ Copy the Prompt

Tap Copy Prompt and paste it into your favourite AI assistant like ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Grok, Microsoft Copilot, or other AI services.

3️⃣ Browse AI Tools

Open the AI Tools section to discover useful AI tools and official AI websites for writing, coding, image generation, video creation, productivity, learning, and much more.

4️⃣ Read AI News

Tap any news source to open the official website and read the latest updates directly from the publisher.

Stay updated with the latest announcements from OpenAI, Google AI, Anthropic, Microsoft AI, Meta AI, xAI, NVIDIA AI, Hugging Face and other leading AI companies.

5️⃣ Search

Use the Search feature to quickly find AI tools, prompt templates, and resources without scrolling through the app.

6️⃣ Save Favorites

Tap the ⭐ button to save prompts or tools you use frequently. Open the Favorites page anytime to access them quickly.

7️⃣ Settings

Use Settings to change the app theme, access Privacy Policy, About page, and other useful options.

8️⃣ Internet Required

Most AI tools and news open official websites. An internet connection is required for these features.

9️⃣ Privacy

AI Tools Hub does not send your prompts or conversations to AI providers. When you open external AI services like ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Grok or Microsoft Copilot, your interaction happens directly with that provider and is governed by their own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

💡 Tips

  • Use clear topics for better prompts.
  • Save useful prompts in Favorites.
  • Explore AI News regularly.
  • Try different AI assistants.
  • Share AI Tools Hub with friends.

🎉 Enjoy Exploring AI

Thank you for using AI Tools Hub. We regularly add new AI tools, prompt templates, and useful resources to help you stay productive and up to date with the latest developments in Artificial Intelligence.
