AI Tools Hub - Data Deletion

AI Tools Hub does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, email address, phone number, or payment information to use the app.

How to Request Data Deletion

If you would like to request deletion of data associated with your use of AI Tools Hub, please send an email to:

ashokashkashas@gmail.com

Please include "AI Tools Hub - Data Deletion Request" in the subject of your email and describe the data or information you would like deleted.

What Data May Be Deleted

Depending on the request, we will delete or remove data associated with the use of AI Tools Hub that we are able to identify and control, including applicable analytics-related data where deletion is supported.

Learning progress and quiz information stored locally on your device can be removed by clearing the app's data from Android settings or uninstalling the app.

Analytics Data

AI Tools Hub uses Google Analytics for Firebase to help understand app usage, performance, stability, and user experience. Where applicable, analytics data deletion requests will be handled using the deletion capabilities provided by Google Analytics and Firebase.

Data Retention

Analytics data collected through Google Analytics for Firebase may be retained according to Google's applicable data retention policies and settings. Where deletion is requested and technically supported, we will take appropriate steps to delete or anonymize the applicable data.

Data stored locally on the user's device, such as learning progress and quiz information, remains on the device until the user clears the app's data or uninstalls the app.

Some data may be retained for a limited period where necessary for legitimate legal, security, or technical purposes.

Processing of Requests

We will review valid deletion requests and take appropriate steps to delete or anonymize applicable data that we control, subject to technical limitations and any data that must be retained for legitimate legal or security purposes.
